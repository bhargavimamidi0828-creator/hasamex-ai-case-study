Dr. Jean Martin, Head of Urology (France)

Adoption of robotic surgery in France is growing, but it remains concentrated in larger academic hospitals and private centres. The market is not yet broad-based, and the strongest uptake is still in high-volume specialist settings where the economics are easier to justify.

The main barriers remain capital approval and return on investment. Decision-makers are looking carefully at utilisation, procedure volume, maintenance costs, and the overall business case before approving a purchase. Training multiple surgeons is also important, because a system only yields value if it is used consistently across a team rather than by one specialist alone.

He expects steady growth in adoption over time and indicates a typical purchase timeline of around 6 to 12 months, especially when the hospital already has a clear clinical case and a plan for utilisation.
