Dr. Emily Carter, Consultant Urologist (United Kingdom)

In the UK, adoption is increasing, and robotic surgery is becoming standard for selected procedures in some larger NHS trusts. Access is still variable, though, and not all hospitals have the same level of availability or capability.

She places strong emphasis on training capacity alongside funding. Even when money is available, the real challenge is whether the hospital can train enough surgeons and build a sustainable operating model. She also frames the economics and clinical strategy as balanced: the decision is not only about cost, but also about service model, outcomes, and patient pathways.

She expects adoption to continue to rise, with a typical procurement or implementation timeline of around 6 to 9 months when funding is already available. In other words, the combination of budget readiness and training capacity is critical to faster adoption.
