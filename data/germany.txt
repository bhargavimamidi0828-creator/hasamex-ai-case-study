Anna Keller, Former Hospital Procurement Director (Germany)

In Germany, adoption is uneven. Larger university hospitals are ahead of the market, while smaller and mid-sized hospitals move more slowly. This creates a mixed picture where the technology is accepted in advanced centres but still limited outside those settings.

The key barriers are cost and proving sufficient utilisation. Procurement teams consider total cost of ownership, procedure volume, maintenance, service contracts, and training requirements before approving a purchase. In practice, a hospital needs to be confident that the system will be used enough to justify the investment over the full lifecycle.

She describes growth as gradual rather than rapid and gives a purchase timeline of approximately 9 to 18 months, depending on financing, procurement process, and the ability to demonstrate volume and training readiness.
